##  CUSTOM PITCH BUILDER

**Use this template to build custom pitches:**

```
TEMPLATE: [Partner Name] Pitch

1. ATTENTION (Hook - 1 sentence)
   └─ "What if [Partner Type] could [major benefit]?"

2. PROBLEM (Their pain point - 2 sentences)
   ├─ Current state
   └─ Impact/cost

3. SOLUTION (Trayon + Partnership - 2 sentences)
   ├─ What we provide
   └─ How partnership works

4. BENEFIT (Why they benefit - 2 sentences)
   ├─ Financial (cost save/revenue gain)
   └─ Strategic (competitive advantage)

5. PROOF (Why this works - 1-2 bullets)
   ├─ Technology proven
   └─ Credentials/partnerships

6. CALL TO ACTION (Next step - 1 sentence)
   └─ "Let's schedule a 30-min discovery call"

Total: 90 seconds
```
